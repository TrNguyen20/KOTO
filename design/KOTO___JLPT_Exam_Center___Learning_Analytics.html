Section II: Grammar & Sentence Patterns (文法・文の組み立て) Question 18 of 35
QUESTION 18 · INCORRECT Time Spent: 58 seconds (Threshold: 45s)
田中さんは 毎朝、新宿駅 [ ? ] 電車を 降ります。
"Mr. Tanaka gets off the train at Shinjuku Station every morning."
[A] に (ni) close Your Selection

Common False Friend: Learners frequently associate stations with に, confusing the destination of boarding (電車に 乗る) with the locus of detachment.

[B] で (de) Distractor

で marks an action realm, but the motion verb 降りる grammaticalizes departure from a bounded physical space.

[C] を (wo) check Correct Answer

Crucial Rule: Verbs of detachment and egress (降りる, 出る, 離れる) require を to mark the origin space departed.

[D] へ (he) Distractor

Directional vector particle. Cannot govern point-of-origin motion.
