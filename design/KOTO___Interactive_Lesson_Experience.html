history_edu Phonological History

Orthographic Preservation: 歴史的仮名遣い

In Old Japanese (Heian Period), the particle は was pronounced with a bilabial stop /pa/, which weakened phonologically through /ɸa/ (fa), settling into /wa/. In the 1946 orthographic post-war reform, the glyph は was retained exclusively as the grammatical topic marker to honor classical grammatical cohesion.
