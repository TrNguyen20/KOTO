Analyzed Segment 「一人でお願いします」
一人 (ひとり) Noun / Counter

Irregular counter reading. (Not *いちにん). Designates party unit size.

で (Particle) Scope / State

Condition & scope marker: indicates acting "as a solo individual" or "as a single unit".

お願いします Formulaic Polite

Polite petition featuring the honorific prefix お- (o-negai-shimasu).

psychology_alt Why 「一人で」 instead of 「一人を」?

Unlike English ("table for one"), Japanese treats 一人 as the state or agency in which the request is made. 「で」 establishes this perimeter of action.
