## Brand & Style
The design system positions itself at the intersection of traditional Japanese publishing aesthetics and high-performance digital SaaS. Designed for discerning learners progressing from JLPT N5 through N1, it actively rejects juvenile gamification tropes—no neon streaks, mascots, or frantic counters. Instead, it cultivates an atmosphere of deep focus, intellectual rigor, and serene craftsmanship (*shokunin kishitsu*).

Visual rhythm is driven by asymmetrical editorial balance, generous breathing space, and subtle, tactile framing reminiscent of tactile washi stationery, sumi calligraphy, and archival dictionaries. Interactions feel measured, intentional, and quiet, instilling confidence and sustained concentration.

## Colors
The palette derives directly from traditional Japanese materials:

- **Primary (*Shuiro* / Vermilion Crimson - `#D1382A`, deep `#BA2E22`):** Used deliberately for key focal actions, seal-like badges, critical alerts, and focused review states. Never applied across large surface floods.
- **Secondary (*Aizome* / Deep Indigo - `#1D3557`, soft `#2A4365`):** Serves grammatical framing, kanji etymology panels, structured meta-information, and secondary controls.
- **Tertiary (*Matcha* / Muted Tea Green - `#386641`):** Reserved exclusively for retention states, JLPT mastery markers, retention confirmations, and steady progression milestones.
- **Neutrals (*Sumi* Ink & *Washi* Paper):**
  - Background Base: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Washi Ivory)
  - Surface Substrate: `#F4EFEB` (Muted Rice Paper)
  - Surface Raised / Card: `#FFFFFF`
  - Structural Borders: `#E8E3DC` (Fine Washi Grain)
  - Muted Meta Text: `#8C867A` (Aged Ink Tone)
  - Text Primary: `#161618` (Pure Deep Sumi Ink)
  - Text Secondary: `#242428` (Diluted Sumi)

## Typography
Typography is paired deliberately to support seamless bilingual study:

- **Headlines & Kanji Characters:** Rendered using **Noto Serif** (with Japanese glyph fallback *Noto Serif JP* / *Hiragino Mincho ProN*). This honors the brush-weight nuance of kanji radicals and brings literary weight to lesson milestones and contextual citations.
- **Interface & Explanatory Body:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** (with Japanese fallback *Noto Sans JP* / *Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN*). It provides unembellished, highly legible explanations, grammatical notes, and pronunciation guides.
- **Furigana / Ruby Annotations:** Positioned strictly at 50% scale of the target character, vertically centered above kanji clusters with explicit line-height compensation to avoid clipping.

## Layout & Spacing
Layouts use a structured 12-column system on desktop and tablet, resolving into a 4-column stack on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Centered max content container of `1280px` with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Layouts favor editorial two-page splits: study material / source text on the primary span (7–8 columns) and morphological breakdown / vocabulary notes on the side panel (4–5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Side panels collapse into progressive disclosure sheets or stacked blocks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Padding prioritizes edge clearance for high-density lexical entries.
- **Whitespace (*Ma*):** Generous inter-module spacing (`space-xl`) prevents cognitive overload during prolonged recall sessions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids simulated heavy physical drop-shadows in favor of architectural layering and fine lines:

- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:** Primary surfaces rely on a crisp `1px solid #E8E3DC` hairline border. Interactive elements remain planar and visually rooted in the washi background.
- **Tonal Substrates:** Higher priority surfaces shift from `#FBF9F5` (canvas base) to `#FFFFFF` (elevated review cards, reading drawers).
- **Subtle Ambient Lift:** Where focus isolation is mandatory (such as modal study overlays, radical selectors, or flash inspection drawers), apply an ethereal, warm sumi shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 22, 24, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 22, 24, 0.03)`. No pure black, high-opacity cast shadows are permitted.

## Shapes
Forms adhere to a soft, disciplined geometry (`roundedness: 1` / `0.25rem` base border radius).

Corners avoid harsh clinical points while deliberately avoiding pillowy, toy-like curves. Standard interactive elements (input fields, card containers, tags) utilize `0.25rem` (4px). Larger containers and dialog sheets use `0.5rem` (8px). 

Circular elements are strictly reserved for seal motifs (*hanko* approval stamps), index indicators (e.g., JLPT level badges like "N1"), and audio toggle triggers.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#161618` (Sumi Black) background with `#FBF9F5` text, or `#D1382A` (Vermilion) for definitive commit states ("Complete Review"). 4px radius, padded with `0.75rem` vertical by `1.5rem` horizontal.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent background bounded by a `1px solid #E8E3DC` border, transitioning to `#F4EFEB` background on hover.
  - *Text:* Unbordered with subtle underline offset for vocabulary drill skip actions.
- **JLPT / Progress Chips:**
  - Compact badges carrying `1px solid #E8E3DC`, `#F4EFEB` background, and small uppercase typography. Level tags (e.g., N3, N2, N1) utilize muted indigo (`#1D3557`) or matcha (`#386641`) accents.
- **Reading & Flash Cards:**
  - White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline border (`#E8E3DC`). Ample inner padding (`space-xl`). Primary kanji glyph centered at `kanji-hero` scale, followed by quiet, muted metadata in aged ink tones.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Clean ivory background (`#F4EFEB`) framed by 1px `#E8E3DC` stroke. On focus, smoothly transitions border to sumi black (`#161618`) without thick outer rings or aggressive glow effects.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Square or circular markers bounded by `#8C867A`, shifting to solid `#161618` with a crisp white check upon selection.
- **Vocabulary & Grammar Lists:**
  - Clean editorial tables with hairline horizontal dividers (`#E8E3DC`). Furigana rendered inline with semantic tags. Zero alternating row zebra stripes; hierarchy is maintained strictly through typographic contrast.
- **Hanko Seal Badge (System-Specific Component):**
  - A square, stamp-inspired vermilion badge (`#D1382A` border and text) placed subtly to authenticate mastery, daily completion, or perfect recall strings.
